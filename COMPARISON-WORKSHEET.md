## 4. If comparing an AI hairstyle

- Inspect hair length, volume and silhouette.
- Check whether the edit also changed eyes, skin, jaw or other facial details.
- Save the idea with a note identifying it as an AI concept.
- Ask what is achievable with your actual hair and maintenance preferences.

AI imagery can change facial details. It is a concept for discussion, not a guaranteed haircut outcome.
